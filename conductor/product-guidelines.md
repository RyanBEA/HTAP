# Product Guidelines

## Communication Style
- **Technical and Concise:** Documentation and commit messages should prioritize precision, technical accuracy, and brevity.

## Testing Strategy
- **Comprehensive Coverage:** High automated test coverage is required for core logic, with a specific focus on the accuracy of NBC compliance calculations and data extractions.

## Development Priorities
- **Strict Compliance:** The highest priority is ensuring absolute accuracy and adherence to the 2020 NBC (and subsequent versions) requirements for compliance reporting.
